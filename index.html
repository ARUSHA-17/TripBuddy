About this adventure

Reconnect with your inner self in the heart of Ubud. This 8-day spiritual retreat is designed for those seeking a balance between high-altitude adventure and deep meditative practices. We'll start our days with sunrise yoga overlooking the Tegallalang Rice Terrace and end them with traditional Balinese sound healing ceremonies.

Planned Itinerary

01 Arrival & Opening Ceremony

Airport pickup, check-in to our private villa, and a traditional welcome dinner. We'll set our intentions for the week under the first moonrise.

02 Ubud Jungle Trek & Meditation

Early morning guided trek through Campuhan Ridge Walk, followed by midday meditation session and healthy organic lunch.

03 Water Purification Ritual

Visit Tirta Empul Temple for authentic holy spring water ritual with local spiritual masters.
